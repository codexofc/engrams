# Support SLAs before 2026

The grid that applied until 2025-12-31, replaced by [[support-sla-tiers]] when the Enterprise plan and the shared channels were introduced. Kept to read the 2025 tickets and the Q4 2025 breach report.

## The grid

Two plans only, Standard and Plus. Standard: first response within 24 business hours, no resolution target. Plus: first response within 8 business hours, resolution "best effort within 5 business days". Business hours Monday to Friday 9:00 to 17:00 CET, French public holidays. Channel: the Deskline form and e-mail; no chat, no phone, no shared channel.

The clock stopped at any human reply, including a macro asking for identifiers. This is the rule HF-3002 changed: in 2025 a `need-reference` reply counted as a response, which made the numbers look better than the service was. Measured on Q3 2025 with the old rule, Plus first response median was 3 h 10; recomputed with the 2026 rule on the same tickets, 4 h 50.

## Why it was replaced

Three shippers signing in Q4 2025 asked for a one-hour response and a named contact, which did not exist. The Enterprise tier was created for them, with the extended Saturday window and the shared channel. Standard became Starter, Plus became Business with the 4-hour target, and the resolution targets became contractual instead of best effort.

The 5 % credit clause on repeated breaches did not exist in 2025. The Q4 2025 webhook outage at a metals shipper, which would have triggered it, was handled with a goodwill gesture by the account manager instead, and is the reason the clause was written down.

## What did not change

The French holiday calendar for everyone, the Deskline tooling, and the rule that feature requests carry no SLA.
